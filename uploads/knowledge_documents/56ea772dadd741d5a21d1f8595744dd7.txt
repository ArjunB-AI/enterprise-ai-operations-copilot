1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
====================================================================================================

1.1 Purpose
-----------

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines how employees of Arjun Tech AI Pvt. Ltd. (the
Company) request, receive, use, replace and return Company IT assets. It explains when a faulty or
old asset is repaired or replaced, who must approve the replacement, and how the asset record is
kept correct.

1.2 Scope
---------

This SOP applies to all Company-owned IT assets issued to employees, interns and contract staff, at
the Bahadurpally, Hyderabad office and for remote work. It covers laptops, desktops, monitors,
keyboards and mice, headsets, docking stations and other approved peripherals. It does not cover
mobile phones, office furniture or personally owned equipment.

4. ASSET ASSIGNMENT PROCEDURE
====================================================================================================

  1) New joiner assets are requested in the onboarding ticket (ATAI-HR-IT-SOP-004). Other requests
    for new assets are created in the ServiceDesk Portal as Service Request, category New asset,
    with the business reason and Department Manager name.
  2) The IT Support Engineer checks the request against the employee's role and the standard
    specification.
  3) The IT Asset Coordinator checks stock in the IT Asset Register. If the item is in stock, it is
    reserved for the employee. If it is not in stock, the IT Asset Coordinator informs the IT
    Support Manager, who decides whether to purchase with Finance Manager budget confirmation.
  4) The IT Administrator prepares laptops and desktops: standard image, disk encryption, device
    management enrolment, security tools and the employee's baseline software.
  5) The IT Asset Coordinator updates the register: status changes from Available to Assigned, with
    the assignee's name, employee ID, department and assignment date.
  6) The employee collects the asset at the Bahadurpally IT Support desk (or receives it through an
    agreed courier process for remote staff). The employee shows identification and signs the asset
    acknowledgement in the ServiceDesk Portal, confirming the asset ID and condition.
  7) The IT Support Engineer closes the request after the employee confirms that the asset works.

Fictional fulfilment targets: replacement peripheral in stock, 2 Business Days; laptop in stock, 3
Business Days; both from completion of approvals.

====================================================================================================
5. ASSET REGISTRATION AND TRACKING
====================================================================================================

5.1 What the Register Holds
---------------------------

Each asset record contains: asset ID, type, make and model, serial number, purchase date, warranty
end date, current status, assigned employee and employee ID, department, location (Bahadurpally
office or remote), date assigned, previous owners, maintenance history, replacement tickets and the
date and reason of retirement.

5.2 Tagging
-----------

  - Each asset receives a physical label showing the asset ID. The label must not be removed.
  - Serial numbers are checked against the register at assignment and return.
  - A fictional example record: Asset ID ATAI-LAP-00125, type Laptop - Developer, status Assigned,
    department Engineering, assigned date 12 January 2026, warranty end 11 January 2029. Another
    fictional example: Asset ID ATAI-MON-00045, type Monitor 27-inch, status Available, location IT
    stock room, Bahadurpally.

5.3 Register Accuracy
---------------------

  - The IT Asset Coordinator updates the register on the same day as every assignment, return,
    status change or retirement.
  - A physical verification of all assets is done every 6 months. Employees confirm the assets
    assigned to them through the ServiceDesk Portal. Missing or unconfirmed assets are raised as
    tickets for investigation.
  - Only the IT Asset Coordinator and the IT Support Manager may change status to Retired.

====================================================================================================
6. ASSET REPLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY
====================================================================================================

An asset is eligible for replacement when one of the following is true. Details of approvals are in
section 9.

  1) Hardware failure that cannot be repaired within the repair target, or where repair cost is more
    than 50 percent of the cost of an equivalent replacement.
  2) Physical damage that makes the asset unsafe or unusable, where repair is not practical.
  3) Performance limitation that is proven: the asset cannot run Company-approved software needed
    for the role after troubleshooting, memory or storage upgrade and re-imaging.
  4) End-of-Life: the asset has reached its standard refresh cycle (section 3) or has no vendor
    support.
  5) Lost or stolen asset, after the loss is reported under section 12.

An asset is not eligible for replacement for personal preference, cosmetic wear, a wish for a newer
model, or when the problem is a software issue that can be fixed. A Department Manager may ask the
IT Support Manager for an exception, which is recorded in the ticket.

Fictional repair target: a repair should be expected to be completed within 5 Business Days. If the
vendor estimate is longer than 5 Business Days, replacement is considered.

====================================================================================================
7. REPLACEMENT REQUEST WORKFLOW
====================================================================================================

7.1 Overview
------------

The workflow has seven steps. Priorities and response targets follow ATAI-IT-POL-001. Most hardware
tickets are P3 or P4. A laptop failure that completely blocks one employee from a critical task may
be raised to P2 by the IT Support Manager (ATAI-IT-POL-001, section 6.5).

7.2 Step 1: Employee Reports an Issue
-------------------------------------

The employee creates a ServiceDesk Portal ticket (Incident, Hardware, with the asset ID) or contacts
IT Support by helpline or walk-up desk. For a lost or stolen device the employee follows section 12
first. The employee must give the asset ID, the symptoms, when the problem began and what has been
tried.

7.3 Step 2: IT Support Performs Initial Troubleshooting
-------------------------------------------------------

The IT Support Engineer runs standard checks: power and charger, battery, connections, safe-mode
start, operating system recovery, diagnostics, storage and memory health, and a replacement of
simple accessories such as cables. The Engineer records the results and the diagnostic reference in
the ticket. If the device cannot be used, the Engineer may issue a Loaner Device from stock (section
7.8). The employee must not be left without a working device where stock allows.

7.4 Step 3: IT Support Determines Repair or Replacement
-------------------------------------------------------

The IT Support Engineer uses the decision table in section 9.2:

  - If the fault can be fixed in the first line, the Engineer repairs it and resolves the ticket.
  - If a vendor repair is needed, the IT Asset Coordinator arranges it. The asset status changes to
    Under Maintenance.
  - If repair is not possible, too slow or too costly, the Engineer recommends replacement and the
    status changes to Replacement Requested.

7.5 Step 4: Department Manager Approval Where Required
------------------------------------------------------

The ServiceDesk Portal routes the replacement to the approvers shown in the approval matrix (section
9.1). Approvers answer within 2 Business Days. A rejected request is closed with a written reason
and the employee is informed.

7.6 Step 5: IT Asset Team Verifies Availability
-----------------------------------------------

The IT Asset Coordinator checks the register for a suitable asset with status Available. Where none
is in stock, the IT Support Manager approves an order after the Finance Manager confirms the budget.
The ticket stays In Progress with a "Waiting for Vendor" note, and the employee keeps the Loaner
Device.

7.7 Step 6: Replacement Is Assigned
-----------------------------------

The IT Administrator prepares the replacement as in section 4 and restores the employee's approved
data from Company cloud storage and backup. The IT Asset Coordinator changes the replacement's
status to Assigned and records the new assignment. The employee signs the acknowledgement.

7.8 Loaner Devices
------------------

  - A Loaner Device is issued only for a laptop or desktop failure that blocks work, while the
    original is repaired or replaced.
  - It has the standard image and is registered in the register as Assigned with the note "Loaner".
  - The Loaner Device must be returned within 2 Business Days after the replacement is assigned.
  - Loaner Devices are issued only after the identity of the employee is verified.

7.9 Step 7: Old Asset Is Returned and Status Updated
----------------------------------------------------

  - The employee returns the old asset (with charger and accessories issued with it) to the IT
    Support desk within 2 Business Days of receiving the replacement.
  - The IT Administrator wipes the device securely after confirming that no data needs to be kept.
    For a device that is in an investigation, no wipe is done until the Information Security Lead
    releases it.
  - The IT Asset Coordinator updates the register: Under Maintenance (if it will be repaired),
    Available (if it is serviceable and reusable) or Retired (if it has reached EOL or cannot be
    repaired).
  - The IT Support Engineer resolves the ticket after both the new asset acknowledgement and the old
    asset return are recorded. The ticket then closes under ATAI-IT-POL-001.

====================================================================================================
8. REPLACEMENT REASONS
====================================================================================================

8.1 Hardware Failure
--------------------

Failure of components without employee misuse, such as battery, motherboard, drive, screen panel or
charger faults. If the asset is within warranty, the vendor warranty route is used first.
Replacement is approved when the repair target would be missed or the fault returns after repair.

8.2 Physical Damage
-------------------

Damage from drops, liquids, pressure or other accidents. The IT Support Engineer records the cause
given by the employee, photographs the damage and checks whether it is accidental or caused by
careless use. A first accidental incident is replaced or repaired normally. A second incident in 12
months, or damage that appears intentional or the result of ignoring safety rules, is reviewed by
the Department Manager and the HR Manager. Whether any cost recovery applies is decided by the HR
Manager under the Company's HR rules, which are not part of this SOP.

8.3 Performance Limitations
---------------------------

The device works but cannot run approved software for the employee's role with acceptable
performance, for example a laptop that cannot run an AI model training workload that the role
requires. The IT Support Engineer first checks background software, storage, memory upgrade and the
image. If the limit remains, the case needs written evidence (resource monitor results and the
software requirement) and approval by the Department Manager and the IT Support Manager. A device
with higher specification than standard needs Head of IT and Security approval.

8.4 End-of-Life Devices
-----------------------

The IT Asset Coordinator produces a quarterly list of assets reaching their refresh cycle in the
next 3 months. The IT Asset Coordinator informs the Department Manager and schedules replacements,
usually within 30 days of the cycle date. No employee request is needed.

8.5 Lost or Stolen Devices
--------------------------

Handled first as a security incident under section 12. Replacement is considered only after the
Information Security Lead confirms that security containment is complete.

--------------------

Retired devices are wiped to remove data before disposal. The IT Asset Coordinator records the

carried out through an approved disposal or recycling vendor chosen by the Operations and
Administration department. Devices held for investigation are not disposed of until the Information
Security Lead releases them.

====================================================================================================
11. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES FOR ASSET SAFETY
====================================================================================================

  - Keep the laptop with you or locked away. Do not leave it in a car, on a desk overnight, or
    unattended in a public place.
  - Use only the supplied charger and approved accessories.
  - Keep drinks and food away from devices.
  - Use a sleeve or bag when travelling. Do not check a laptop into airline luggage.
  - Lock the screen whenever you leave the device, and sign out of sensitive applications.
  - Do not install unapproved software, change disk encryption settings, remove the asset label or
    open the device.
  - Do not lend your device to relatives, friends or colleagues.
  - Do not store the only copy of important work on the laptop. Use the approved Company cloud
    storage.
  - Do not use a Company laptop for personal activities that increase security risk, such as
    downloading pirated software.
  - Report any damage, fault, loss or suspicious behaviour of the device at once (ATAI-IT-POL-001).
  - Work on a personal device is not permitted for restricted Company data.

====================================================================================================
12. LOST OR STOLEN ASSET REPORTING PROCEDURE
====================================================================================================

12.1 Employee Actions
---------------------

  1) Report the loss or theft immediately by calling the IT Support helpline, and in any case within
    1 hour of discovering it. Do not wait to search for the device.
  2) Create a ServiceDesk Portal ticket (Incident, Security, Lost or stolen device) as soon as
    possible, giving the asset ID, the place and time of loss, what data or access the device held,
    and whether the device was locked, encrypted and signed in.
  3) Change the password of any account used on the device from another trusted device, if you can.
    Deny any unexpected MFA prompts.
  4) Follow any instruction from the Information Security Lead about reporting a theft to the
    police. The Company may ask for a copy of the police complaint number for its records.
  5) Do not try to recover the device from unknown persons or attempt to access it remotely
    yourself.

12.2 IT Support and Security Actions
------------------------------------

  1) The IT Support Engineer creates or updates the ticket, sets the priority to at least P2, and
    notifies the Information Security Lead and the IT Support Manager immediately.
  2) The Information Security Lead takes ownership and follows the security incident workflow in
    ATAI-SEC-POL-005. A device with unencrypted restricted data, privileged credentials or access to
    production systems is treated as P1.
  3) The IT Administrator locks the device remotely, or wipes it when the Information Security Lead
    confirms, and revokes the sessions, tokens, VPN profile and MFA registrations linked to the
    device. Target: within 1 hour of confirmation.
  4) The IT Administrator reviews the sign-in logs for the employee accounts and disables access
    that is not needed.
  5) The IT Asset Coordinator changes the asset status to Replacement Requested and notes "Reported
    lost" or "Reported stolen". If the device is not recovered after 10 Business Days, the status
    changes to Retired with the reason "Lost" or "Stolen".
  6) The Finance Manager is informed so that the Company's fixed-asset records are updated. The HR
    Manager is informed if negligence is suspected.
  7) After the Information Security Lead confirms that containment is complete, a replacement is
    issued under section 7 with approvals as in section 9.
  8) If the device is recovered, it is not returned to the employee's use until the IT Administrator
    has checked it and the Information Security Lead has released it.

13. ASSET RETURN DURING EMPLOYEE SEPARATION
====================================================================================================

  1) The HR Manager submits the offboarding request with the last working day (ATAI-HR-IT-SOP-004,
    section 20).
  2) The IT Asset Coordinator reviews the register for all assets assigned to the employee and sends
    the list to the employee and the Department Manager.
  3) The employee returns all assets with accessories to the IT Support desk on or before the last
    working day. Remote employees return them through the agreed courier process, within 3 Business
    Days of the last working day.
  4) The IT Support Engineer inspects each asset against the acknowledgement record, checks the
    serial numbers and notes damage or missing items.
  5) The IT Administrator wipes the devices (after any legal or investigation hold is confirmed
    absent) and the IT Asset Coordinator changes the status to Available or Retired.
  6) Missing or damaged assets are reported to the HR Manager and Department Manager. The HR Manager
    decides on follow-up under the Company's HR rules.
  7) The IT Support Engineer records in the offboarding ticket that all assets have been received.
    The access removal steps in ATAI-SEC-POL-002 (section 13) run in parallel.

14. SAMPLE ASSET REPLACEMENT SCENARIOS
====================================================================================================

All names, IDs and cases below are fictional examples.

14.1 Scenario A: Laptop Will Not Power On
-----------------------------------------

An AI/ML engineer reports that laptop ATAI-LAP-00188 does not power on. Ticket ATAI-INC-000731 is
raised as P3, and raised to P2 by the IT Support Manager because the engineer has a model release
today. The IT Support Engineer tests the charger and battery reset and finds a failed motherboard.
Vendor repair is estimated at 8 Business Days, longer than 5. The Engineer issues a Loaner Device
and recommends replacement. The IT Support Manager approves (hardware failure needs no Department
Manager approval). The IT Asset Coordinator assigns a developer laptop from stock in 2 Business
Days. The old laptop is returned and its status changes to Retired.

14.2 Scenario B: Monitor Flickering
-----------------------------------

An operations employee reports that monitor ATAI-MON-00045 flickers. The IT Support Engineer swaps
the cable and tests the port, but the fault remains. A replacement monitor is issued from stock the
next day. The faulty monitor is returned and marked Under Maintenance, then Retired when the vendor
says it is not repairable. No approval is needed.

14.3 Scenario C: Laptop Dropped and Screen Cracked
--------------------------------------------------

A finance employee drops the laptop and the screen cracks. The IT Support Engineer photographs the
damage. This is the first incident in 12 months, so the IT Support Manager approves a screen repair
because it is practical (4 Business Days). A Loaner Device is lent. The status goes Assigned, Under
Maintenance, Assigned. If the same employee causes another incident within 12 months, the Department
Manager and HR Manager review the case.

14.4 Scenario D: Slow Laptop for Data Work
------------------------------------------

A data analyst in AI/ML says the laptop is too slow. The IT Support Engineer clears startup software
and finds memory use at the limit while running the approved training notebook. A memory upgrade is
not supported. The Engineer collects the resource monitor evidence. The Department Manager and IT
Support Manager approve a standard developer laptop. The Head of IT and Security does not need to
approve, because the specification is standard. The old device is returned and set to Available
after the wipe, or Retired if it has reached EOL.

14.5 Scenario E: Laptop Reaches End-of-Life
-------------------------------------------

The IT Asset Coordinator's quarterly report shows that laptop ATAI-LAP-00125 reaches the 4-year
cycle in January 2030. (Fictional dates.) The IT Asset Coordinator informs the Department Manager 3
months before and schedules the replacement. The employee exchanges the device during a planned slot
with no ticket needed.

14.6 Scenario F: Laptop Stolen From a Car
-----------------------------------------

An engineer reports by helpline, within 20 minutes of discovery, that a laptop was stolen from a
parked car. Ticket ATAI-INC-000790 is created as Security, Lost or stolen device, P2 minimum. The
Information Security Lead confirms that disk encryption was on and no restricted data was stored
locally, so P2 stays. The IT Administrator locks and wipes the device remotely, revokes tokens and
signs the sessions out within 1 hour of confirmation. The employee's passwords are reset and MFA
re-enrolled under ATAI-SEC-POL-002. The Department Manager and IT Support Manager approve a
replacement after containment. The HR Manager is informed. The asset becomes Retired with the reason
"Stolen" after 10 Business Days if not recovered.

14.7 Scenario G: Employee Resigns and Returns Equipment
-------------------------------------------------------

An employee resigns with a last working day of 30 November 2026 (fictional). The IT Asset
Coordinator lists the laptop, monitor and headset. On the last working day the employee returns
them. The IT Support Engineer inspects them and finds the headset cable missing. The headset is
recorded as damaged, and the HR Manager is informed. The laptop is wiped and set to Available.

15. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
====================================================================================================

Q: My laptop is not working. How do I ask for a replacement?
A: Create a ServiceDesk Portal ticket (Incident, Hardware) with the asset ID. The IT Support
   Engineer will troubleshoot first and then decide whether repair or replacement is appropriate.
   You do not need to ask for a replacement directly, but you can say that you think the device
   cannot be repaired.

Q: How long does it take to get a replacement laptop?
A: The fictional internal target is 3 Business Days from the completion of approvals if a suitable
   laptop is in stock. If none is in stock, the IT Support Manager approves an order and the time
   depends on the vendor. A Loaner Device can be lent meanwhile.

Q: Who needs to approve my replacement?
A: It depends on the reason (see the approval matrix in section 9.1). Hardware failure needs no
   approval from your manager. Performance limitation needs your Department Manager and the IT
   Support Manager. Lost or stolen devices need the Department Manager and the IT Support Manager,
   after the Information Security Lead has handled the security aspects.

Q: Can I get a new laptop because mine is slow?
A: Slowness alone does not qualify. IT Support first troubleshoots. If the device still cannot run
   the approved software for your role, a performance-limitation replacement may be approved with
   your Department Manager and the IT Support Manager.

Q: When is a laptop replaced automatically?
A: When it reaches the standard refresh cycle, which is 4 years for laptops (fictional). The IT
   Asset Coordinator contacts your Department Manager and you before the date.

Q: What do I do if I lose my laptop?
A: Call the IT Support helpline immediately and no later than 1 hour after you notice it is missing.
   Then raise a Security ticket for a lost or stolen device. The Information Security Lead takes
   over. Do not wait until you have searched everywhere.

Q: Will I have to pay for a damaged or lost device?
A: This SOP does not decide that. IT Support only records the facts. For repeated damage or
   negligent loss, the HR Manager decides on any follow-up under the Company's HR rules.

Q: What happens to the data on my old laptop?
A: Store important files in the approved Company cloud storage. IT Support restores approved data to
   the replacement where possible. The old laptop is wiped after return, unless the Information
   Security Lead has placed it on hold.

Q: Do I need to return the old charger and accessories?
A: Yes. Return the device and all accessories that were issued with it within 2 Business Days of
   receiving the replacement, and at the latest on your last working day if you are leaving.

Q: What is a Loaner Device and who can have one?
A: It is a temporary standard device for employees whose assigned laptop or desktop has failed and
   who cannot work. It is issued by IT Support from stock after identity verification and must be
   returned within 2 Business Days after the replacement is given to you.

Q: How can I check which assets are assigned to me?
A: Ask IT Support for your asset list through a ServiceDesk Portal ticket. You also confirm your
   assets during the physical verification that is done every 6 months.

Q: What are the asset statuses?
A: Available, Assigned, Under Maintenance, Replacement Requested and Retired.
